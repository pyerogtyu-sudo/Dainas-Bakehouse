Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our menu, branches and ordering options.

What products do you offer?

Our menu includes chocolate crinkles, cookies, brownies, cakes, cupcakes, cinnamon rolls, breads, pastries, assorted pastry boxes, coffee and other beverages. Browse the Shop page for current listed products and prices.

Are chocolate crinkles available?

The menu features Classic Chocolate, Red Velvet, Matcha, Cookies and Cream, Double Chocolate, and Strawberry Crinkles. Availability may vary; contact the bakehouse to confirm before placing an order.

What flavors of crinkles can I order?

Featured varieties are Classic Chocolate, Red Velvet, Matcha, Cookies and Cream, Double Chocolate, and Strawberry Crinkles. Check the menu for descriptions and current listed prices.

Which crinkles are your best sellers?

Classic Chocolate Crinkles, Red Velvet Crinkles, and Double Chocolate Crinkles are highlighted as featured customer favorites. These are curated marketing selections, not rankings verified against sales records.

How much do your products cost?

Prices are displayed in Philippine pesos on each product card. Current listed examples include crinkle packs from ₱120 to ₱145, assorted pastry boxes at ₱480, and whole cakes from ₱650 to ₱850. Check the Shop page for the price of each item and contact the bakehouse to confirm current availability before ordering.

Do you offer assorted pastry boxes?

An assorted pastry box is listed on the menu. The selection may vary by availability; please contact the bakehouse to confirm the box contents, quantity and lead time before ordering.

Can I place a custom cake order?

For custom cake inquiries, contact the bakehouse with your preferred size, flavor, design, serving date and budget. Customization options, price, availability and lead time must be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Where are your branches located?

The listed branch areas are Zapote, Caloocan City; Sto. Domingo, Cainta, Rizal; and Sta. Mesa, Manila. Map links search these areas and do not mark confirmed storefront pins. Please confirm the exact location before visiting.

What are your operating hours?

The current suggested hours are daily, 8:00 AM–7:00 PM. These hours must be confirmed with the relevant branch before travel, as actual schedules may differ.

Do you accept advance orders?

For cakes, pastry boxes or larger orders, contact the bakehouse ahead of your preferred date. Lead times and acceptance must be confirmed directly before making plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

Please contact the bakehouse to confirm current payment options before arranging an order. This website does not collect payment.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery availability, coverage, fees and timing depend on the order and location. Contact the bakehouse to confirm whether delivery can be arranged before placing an order. An order is not scheduled until the team confirms it directly.

How do I contact the bakery?

Use the contact information on the Contact Us page. Use the contact form to prepare an email inquiry or reach the team directly at the published contact details.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact the bakehouse as soon as possible to request a change or cancellation. Acceptance depends on the order status and preparation stage; confirm the applicable terms with the team before ordering.

How should I store my baked goods?

Storage depends on the product. Keep breads and cookies in a clean, airtight container when appropriate, and refrigerate cream-filled cakes and other perishable items according to the guidance provided with the product. Ask the bakehouse for product-specific storage and shelf-life information.
